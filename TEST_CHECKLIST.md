# Combat HUD 0.6 (beta): test checklist

**Tester:** ____________________  **Date:** ____________

**Device and browser** (for example "iPhone 14, Safari" or "Samsung tablet, Chrome"): ____________________

**What you need:** your character exported from D&D Beyond as a PDF (Manage, then Export to PDF). If you can, test with both a spellcaster and a non-caster. Skip any check that does not apply to your character.

**How to use this:** tick the box when it works as described. If it does not, leave it empty and write what happened under **Notes** for that section.

---

## 1. Install and first launch
- [ ] The app opens from the link in a normal browser (not inside another app)
- [ ] It can be installed (Share or menu, then Add to Home Screen / Install app)
- [ ] The home screen icon opens it full screen
- [ ] The first screen says "Import your character" with no character already loaded

Notes:

## 2. Importing a character
- [ ] Tapping Choose D&D Beyond PDF opens the file picker
- [ ] Picking your PDF adds the character ("Added ...")
- [ ] The header shows the right name, class, level and subclass
- [ ] Armour class, initiative, speed and hit points match D&D Beyond
- [ ] A PDF that is not a character sheet gives a clear message instead of breaking

Notes:

## 3. Status panel (the top or left panel)
- [ ] Type an amount, tap **Damage**: hit points go down
- [ ] Tap **Heal**: hit points go up and stop at your maximum
- [ ] Damage to 0 HP shows death save circles, and they can be tapped
- [ ] Healing from 0 HP clears the death saves
- [ ] Tapping **Initiative** opens a roll
- [ ] Each counter (Second Wind, Rage, Superiority Dice and so on) goes down with **-** and up with **+**
- [ ] **Inspiration** switches on and off
- [ ] (Casters) Spell slot circles can be tapped to spend and regain

Notes:

## 4. Attacking (Combat tab)
- [ ] The Attack group lists your weapons and attack cantrips with the right to-hit numbers
- [ ] Tapping an attack opens a flowchart
- [ ] **Roll to hit** gives a result. Advantage and Disadvantage each roll two dice
- [ ] Choosing **Hit**, **Miss** or **Critical hit** works. A natural 20 selects Critical hit by itself
- [ ] **Roll damage** shows a total that includes your bonus
- [ ] A critical hit doubles the dice, not the bonus
- [ ] (Savage Attacker) Both rolls show, the higher is marked used, the lower is struck through
- [ ] (Sneak Attack) The Extra damage box adds the dice, and works once per turn
- [ ] (Maneuvers) Spending a superiority die adds it to damage and shows the save DC
- [ ] (Maneuvers) Undo gives the die back
- [ ] Weapon mastery reminders (Sap, Slow, Vex and so on) show after a hit
- [ ] If you have Extra Attack, **Next attack (2 of 2)** moves on to the second attack
- [ ] A miss skips the damage steps

Notes:

## 5. Bonus actions, reactions and other actions
- [ ] Bonus action group lists your bonus action features (and is hidden if you have none)
- [ ] A bonus action opens a flowchart, and using it spends the counter
- [ ] (Second Wind) Roll, then Apply: your hit points go up
- [ ] Reaction group lists your reactions and always shows Opportunity attack
- [ ] (Riposte, Feinting Attack, Sentinel) Choosing a weapon starts an attack with the right bonus
- [ ] Other actions lists Action Surge or similar, Grapple or Shove (with a DC), and the standard actions
- [ ] Hide rolls a Stealth check

Notes:

## 6. Turns
- [ ] **New turn** increases the turn number
- [ ] Once-per-turn features show "ready" or "used", and New turn makes them ready again
- [ ] The bar shows a running "dealt" damage total after you hit something

Notes:

## 7. Spells (casters only)
- [ ] The Spells tab shows your save DC and attack bonus (both, if multiclass)
- [ ] Spells are grouped by level, and search finds a spell
- [ ] The filters work (All, Prepared, Cantrips, each level)
- [ ] The star marks a spell as prepared, and prepared bonus action and reaction spells show on the Combat tab
- [ ] Casting a spell spends the slot you pick
- [ ] Casting at a higher level (upcasting) works and changes the dice
- [ ] A ritual spell can be cast without a slot
- [ ] A spell marked 1/long rest can be free cast once
- [ ] Attack spells have a roll button, and save spells show the DC
- [ ] Damage spells (Magic Missile, Burning Hands, Fireball and so on) roll sensible dice
- [ ] Save spells show the half damage for a successful save
- [ ] Healing spells have an **Apply** button that raises your hit points
- [ ] A spell not in the table gives a box to type dice, and rolling works
- [ ] Casting a concentration spell shows a "Concentrating" banner
- [ ] Taking damage while concentrating shows a Constitution save with a DC
- [ ] Roll, Held and Lost each do the right thing, and dropping to 0 HP ends concentration
- [ ] (Sorcerers) The Font of Magic buttons swap slots and Sorcery Points

Notes:

## 8. Stats tab
- [ ] Tapping an ability score rolls a check, and Save rolls a saving throw
- [ ] Tapping a skill rolls it with the right bonus
- [ ] Proficient skills are marked, and expertise is marked differently
- [ ] Hit dice, senses, defenses and proficiencies match your sheet (where you have them)

Notes:

## 9. Features, Gear and Story tabs
- [ ] Features lists your class, species and feat features with their descriptions
- [ ] Gear shows your coins, equipment, how much you carry, and attuned items
- [ ] Story shows your details, traits and backstory (only the parts you filled in)
- [ ] Empty sections are not shown

Notes:

## 10. Resting
- [ ] **Short rest** opens a sheet, and spending a hit die heals you (roll plus Constitution)
- [ ] The short rest sheet lists what will refill, and **Finish short rest** refills it
- [ ] **Long rest** restores hit points, counters and spell slots
- [ ] A long rest gives back about half your hit dice

Notes:

## 11. Combat log
- [ ] The Log tab lists your attacks, damage, spells and rolls in order, grouped by turn
- [ ] The summary numbers (attacks, hits, damage dealt and so on) look right
- [ ] **End combat** moves the fight under Past combats
- [ ] **Copy log** copies text you can paste somewhere
- [ ] **Clear** needs two taps and then empties the current log

Notes:

## 12. Using your own dice
- [ ] The **Dice: app** pill at the top of a sheet switches to "my own"
- [ ] The same setting is in the menu (three dots), under Dice
- [ ] Rolling now asks you to type your numbers instead of rolling
- [ ] A number outside the dice range (for example 9 on a d8) is refused
- [ ] **Roll it for me** rolls just that one roll
- [ ] **Cancel** backs out without spending a die or a counter
- [ ] Damage, a superiority die, healing and a hit die all accept typed numbers
- [ ] A big handful of dice (like 8d6) asks for one total
- [ ] The log shows the numbers you typed

Notes:

## 13. Colours and themes
- [ ] Menu, then Appearance, shows Classic, Air, Fire, Water, Earth and Storm
- [ ] Each one changes the colours straight away
- [ ] Light, Dark and Auto all work
- [ ] Text is easy to read in every theme you try
- [ ] Damage (red) and Heal (green) stand out clearly
- [ ] A second character can have a different theme from the first

Notes:

## 14. Several characters
- [ ] You can add a second character from the menu
- [ ] With two or more characters, opening the app asks "Who are you playing?"
- [ ] Switching characters from the menu works, and each keeps its own hit points
- [ ] Delete needs a second tap
- [ ] "Erase all characters" needs a second tap

Notes:

## 15. After a level up
- [ ] Importing a new PDF of the same character updates them (it says "Updated")
- [ ] The "What changed" list is right
- [ ] Your current hit points and used counters are kept
- [ ] Spell slots and counters pick up any new maximums
- [ ] Your combat log is still there

Notes:

## 16. Offline and updates
- [ ] After using the app once, it still opens with internet switched off
- [ ] Importing a PDF works with internet switched off
- [ ] Closing and reopening the app keeps your character, hit points and counters
- [ ] The menu shows the version number "0.6 (beta)" at the bottom

Notes:

## 17. Layout and feel
- [ ] No sideways scrolling anywhere on a phone
- [ ] It looks right with the phone or tablet held both ways
- [ ] Buttons are easy to tap with a thumb
- [ ] Text is big enough to read at the table
- [ ] Nothing important is hidden behind another element

Notes:

## 18. Guide
- [ ] The user guide page opens and matches what the app actually does
