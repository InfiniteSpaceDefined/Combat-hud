Combat HUD

User guide

A quick-reference combat companion for your phone or tablet. It reads your D&D Beyond character PDF and walks you through your turn: attacks, damage, spells, rests and more. Built for the 2024 rules.

What it does

  • Reads your PDF. Export your character from D&D Beyond, pick the file, done. There is nothing to type in.
  • Shows only what you have. A rogue never sees spell slots. A wizard never sees rage counters. If your sheet has no backstory, there is no empty backstory box.
  • Walks you through each action as a flowchart: roll to hit, hit or miss, damage, extras. You can use the app's dice or your own.
  • Tracks the fiddly bits: hit points, hit dice, spell slots, class counters, concentration and once-per-turn features.
  • Keeps a combat log you can read back after the fight.
  • Works offline once it has loaded, and keeps everything on your own device.
30-second start: open the app, tap Choose D&D Beyond PDF, pick your exported character sheet, then tap an attack on the Combat tab.

Install it

You can use Combat HUD in a normal browser tab, but installing it gives you an icon on your home screen and a full-screen app that works offline.

  1. Open the link in your normal browser (Safari, Chrome or Edge). Not inside another app like Discord or WhatsApp, which use a built-in browser that cannot install it or open the file picker. If that happens, tap "Open in browser".
  2. Install it:
    iPhone or iPad (Safari)Tap Share, then Add to Home Screen, then Add.
    Android (Chrome)Tap the three dots, then Install app (or Add to Home screen).
    Android (Edge)Tap the three dots, then Add to phone (or Add to Home screen).
    Windows (Edge)Tap the three dots, then Apps, then Install this site as an app.
  3. Open it from the new icon. The first time, give it a few seconds to save itself for offline use. After that it needs no internet.
Updates: when a new version is released, open the app twice while you are online and it refreshes itself. If it still looks old, remove the app and add it again from the browser. Your characters are kept unless you clear the site's data.

Add your character

  1. In D&D Beyond, open your character, tap Manage, then Export to PDF.
  2. Open Combat HUD and tap Choose D&D Beyond PDF. Pick the PDF you just saved.
  3. You land on the Combat tab, ready to play.

More than one character

Add more from the menu (the three dots, top right). With two or more characters, the app asks Who are you playing? each time it opens, and tags the one you used last. With one, it opens straight into them. Switch any time from the menu. Delete asks for a second tap so you cannot do it by accident.

After a level up

Export a fresh PDF and import it again from the menu. Combat HUD recognises the same character by name and updates them instead of adding a copy. It shows a What changed list (new features, attacks, spells, slots) and keeps your current hit points, used counters, combat log and starred spells. New spell slots and counter uses are added for you.

If you rename the character in D&D Beyond, the app treats it as a new character. Delete the old one from the menu when you are happy.

The screen at a glance

The panel at the top is always there, whichever tab you are on (on a tablet it sits on the left).

Hit pointsType an amount, then tap Damage or Heal. Temporary hit points soak up damage first. At 0 HP, death save circles appear.
AC, Initiative, SpeedTap Initiative to roll it. Extra speeds (like climbing) show underneath.
CountersAnything with limited uses on your sheet: Second Wind, Superiority Dice, Rage, Sorcery Points, Bardic Inspiration and so on. Tap − to use one, + to get one back. Rests refill them. Counters marked "Other" on D&D Beyond (like Infuse Item) are manual.
Spell slotsCasters only. Tap a circle to spend or regain a slot.
BannersConcentration, and effects such as Innate Sorcery, appear here with an End button.
Inspiration, restsHeroic inspiration toggle, Short rest and Long rest.

The tabs

Combat, Spells, Stats, Features, Gear, Story and Log. Only the tabs that apply to your character appear. Spells shows up for casters only, and Gear only if you have gear or coins.

Combat tab

The top bar shows Turn N, a running total of damage you have dealt, and the status of once-per-turn features (Sneak Attack, Savage Attacker). Tap New turn at the start of each of your turns: it counts the turn and makes those features ready again.

Below are four groups. Tap one to open it: Attack, Bonus action (hidden if you have none), Reaction and Other actions.

Attacking

Tap a weapon or attack cantrip. If you have Extra Attack, the group says how many attacks you get. The flowchart takes you through it:

  1. Roll to hit. Choose Disadvantage, Normal or Advantage and tap Roll, or use your own dice and skip ahead.
  2. Does it hit? Miss, Hit or Critical hit. A natural 20 or 1 is picked for you.
  3. Damage. Rolls your weapon's dice (doubled on a critical hit). With Savage Attacker, both rolls are shown, the higher one is marked as used and the lower one is struck through. It is once per turn, so later attacks that turn roll once.
  4. Extra damage (optional). Sneak Attack, and Arcane Firearm on spell attacks. Sneak Attack needs a finesse or ranged weapon and is once per turn.
  5. Maneuver (optional). Spend a superiority die. Shows the save DC. You get one maneuver per attack, so an attack already started by Feinting Attack or Riposte cannot add another. Undo gives the die back.
  6. On a hit. Reminders for your weapon masteries (Sap, Slow, Vex and so on).
  7. Finish. Moves on to your next attack.
Everything is logged, whether you roll in the app or on the table. If you use physical dice, just tap Hit or Miss.

Bonus actions, reactions and other actions

Each feature opens a short flowchart with its description under About this, then the steps. Counters are spent for you, and greyed-out boxes show what comes next. Some examples:

  • Second Wind: roll the healing, then Apply it to your hit points.
  • Feinting Attack and Riposte: spend a die, then pick the weapon. The die is added to that attack's damage, and Feinting Attack gives advantage.
  • Cunning Action: choose Dash, Disengage or Hide (Hide rolls Stealth for you).
  • Sentinel and opportunity attacks: pick a weapon and attack. Opportunity attack is always in Reaction.
  • Action Surge, Bardic Inspiration, Innate Sorcery and more: use the counter, then follow the steps.

Other actions holds your special features, Cast a spell, Grapple or Shove (with your save DC) and the standard actions: Dash, Disengage, Dodge, Help, Hide, Influence, Ready, Search, Study and Utilize.

Spells

Casters get a Spells tab. Non-casters never see it.

  • Stats and slots. Each class's save DC and attack bonus (multiclass characters see both), plus slots by level. Sorcerers get a Font of Magic panel to convert slots and Sorcery Points.
  • The list. Grouped by level, with search and filters (All, Prepared, Cantrips, each level). Tap the star to mark a spell as prepared. A diamond means always prepared. C is concentration and R is ritual. Duplicate 2014 and 2024 versions are merged.
  • Casting. Tap a spell, then pick the slot level (upcasting is included), cast it as a ritual, or free-cast it if it says 1/long rest. The slot is spent and the cast is logged.
  • Attack or save. Roll the spell attack (with advantage when an effect gives it) or read off the save DC. For a spell known by two classes, both DCs show.
  • Damage and healing. For common spells the dice are worked out for you and scale with the slot you chose. Cantrips scale with your character level.
    • Magic Missile rolls every dart. Scorching Ray gives each ray its own attack roll.
    • Save spells show the half damage on a successful save.
    • Attack spells have a critical hit toggle.
    • Cure Wounds and False Life have an Apply button for your own hit points or temporary hit points.
    • A spell not in the built-in table gets a box where you type the dice (like 4d6) and roll.
  • Concentration. Casting a concentration spell shows a banner. When you take damage, you get a Constitution save prompt with the right DC, and buttons for Roll, Held and Lost. Dropping to 0 HP ends it.

Prepared bonus action and reaction spells also show up in those groups on the Combat tab, so Shield or Misty Step are one tap away.

For classes that prepare spells (like artificers and wizards), the PDF lists everything available and not what you have prepared. Star the ones you prepared.

Stats, Features, Gear and Story

These tabs cover the rest of your PDF. Anything you left blank on D&D Beyond is left out here.

StatsAbility scores and modifiers (tap to roll a check), saving throws (tap to roll), all 18 skills with proficiency and expertise markers (tap to roll), passive scores, hit dice, senses, defenses, saving throw notes and proficiencies.
FeaturesEvery class, species and feat feature from your sheet, grouped and expandable, with the full description.
GearCoins, how much you are carrying against your limit, attuned magic items and your equipment list.
StoryDetails (age, eyes, hair, faith and so on), personality traits, ideals, bonds, flaws, appearance, allies, backstory and notes.

Rests and resources

Short rest

Tap Short rest. A sheet opens where you can spend hit dice (each heals its roll plus your Constitution modifier, and cannot go above your maximum). It lists what will refill, such as Superiority Dice or Action Surge. Tap Finish short rest to apply it. If you were in a fight, it is saved to the log.

Long rest

Tap Long rest and it restores your hit points, spell slots, counters and free casts, gives back half your hit dice (minimum one), ends concentration and active effects, and saves the current fight to the log.

Combat log

The Log tab fills itself as you play. It records:

  • Each attack: the roll, hit or miss, and a breakdown of the damage (maneuver die, Sneak Attack, Savage Attacker).
  • Spell casts, damage and healing.
  • Feature use, hit points lost and gained, skill and save rolls, and death saves.

Entries are grouped by turn. At the top you get a summary: turns, attacks, hits, misses, damage dealt, your biggest hit, damage taken and healing.

  • End combat saves the fight under Past combats (the last ten are kept).
  • Copy log copies it as text you can paste into your group chat.
  • Clear wipes the current fight (it asks twice).

Resting also saves the current fight automatically.

Colours and themes

Open the menu (three dots), then Appearance.

  • Element colour scheme: Classic, Air (Cool Monochrome), Fire (Midnight Jewel), Water (Ocean Gradient), Earth (Autumn Leaves) or Storm (Noir Neon). Each character has their own, so every player can pick the one that suits them.
  • Light or dark: Auto follows your device. It applies to all your characters.
  • If your sheet mentions exactly one element (for example in the faith or notes field), the menu offers it as a one-tap suggestion.

Privacy and offline

  • Your PDF is read on your device. Nothing is uploaded and there are no accounts.
  • Your characters, log and settings are stored in your browser or installed app on that device only. Another device will not see them.
  • After the first load, the app works with no internet.
  • Clearing the site's data or uninstalling the app erases your characters. Just import the PDFs again to get them back.
  • On iPhone and iPad, avoid clearing Safari's website data if you want to keep your characters.

Troubleshooting

The Choose PDF button does nothingOpen the app in your normal browser or installed app, not inside another app's built-in browser (Discord, Claude, WhatsApp and similar often block file pickers).
"I could not read that PDF"Use the standard character sheet export from D&D Beyond (Manage, then Export to PDF), the one with all the pages. Try exporting again and import the new file.
Spells are missing or not marked as preparedFor classes that prepare spells, the PDF does not say which ones you chose. Star the ones you prepared on the Spells tab.
A number looks wrongThe app reads your sheet, so check it on D&D Beyond first. If it is wrong there, fix it and import again.
It looks out of date after an updateOpen the app twice while online. If that does not help, remove it and add it again from the browser.
My characters disappearedClearing site data removes them. Import your PDFs again.

Not included (yet)

  • A conditions tracker (prone, poisoned and so on).
  • Sharing a battle between several phones. Each device is independent for now, though Copy log helps.
  • Damage dice for every spell. The built-in table covers the common damage and healing spells up to about 5th level, and anything else has the type-in dice box.
  • Arcane Recovery tracks the use but does not pick slots for you. Eldritch Cannon hit points are a manual counter.
  • Editing coins or equipment. The Gear tab shows what was on your PDF at the time.
  • Monsters and enemy hit points.
